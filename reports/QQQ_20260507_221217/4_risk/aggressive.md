In strong index trends, the “perfect” entry often never comes, or it comes after a move so large that waiting becomes a hidden form of underperformance. If this is a portfolio exposure decision, holding a core position while refusing to overcommit is the smart aggressive choice: you preserve upside participation, avoid overpaying for incremental shares, and keep dry powder in case a reset actually appears.
