# Fundamental Report: MDB

## 1) Executive summary

**MDB (MongoDB, Inc.)** remains a high-growth infrastructure software company that is moving closer to durable GAAP profitability while already generating **strong operating cash flow and free cash flow**. The core fundamental picture is:

- **Revenue growth remains strong**: FY2026 revenue reached **$2.464B**, up from **$2.006B** in FY2025, a **22.8% YoY increase**.
- **Losses are narrowing materially**: net loss improved from **-$129.1M** to **-$71.2M**; operating loss improved from **-$216.1M** to **-$137.0M**.
- **Cash generation inflected sharply**: FY2026 operating cash flow was **$505.1M** and free cash flow was **$500.2M**, far above FY2025.
- **Balance sheet is very strong**: MDB ended FY2026 with **$2.387B** in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments against only **$32.9M** of total debt.
- **Major caveat**: stock-based compensation remains very high at **$550.5M** annually, which flatters cash flow relative to true economic dilution.
- **Valuation is still premium**: market cap is about **$23.6B**, forward P/E about **41.6x**, and price/book about **8.0x**. Traders are paying for future margin expansion, not current earnings.

**Bottom line:** MDB’s fundamentals are clearly improving, especially cash generation and balance-sheet quality, but traders should not ignore the premium valuation, high SBC burden, and the fact that reported profitability still depends meaningfully on interest income from its large cash pile.

## 5. Growth sensitivity
A high-beta software stock with premium valuation can re-rate sharply if revenue growth decelerates.
